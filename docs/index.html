Saved You 20 Minutes

Reads a YouTube video's transcript and tells you whether it's worth watching — spoiling the clickbait on purpose.

Hover any thumbnail, click Worth it?, and you get the answer the title was dangling, before you spend the twenty minutes finding out.

SKIP IT

He sold the car after four months because insurance quoted him $6,400/yr, not because of anything mechanical.

The answer is at 18:40. Everything before it is recap and a sponsor read.

What you get

  • The payoff first. If the title asks a question, the first line answers it. If it teases a reveal, the first line reveals it.
  • A verdict. Worth it, skim it, or skip it — judged against the summary you just read, not against nothing.
  • Timestamps that work. Jump straight to the parts that matter, if any do.
  • A clickbait score, when the gap between the title and the video is big enough to be worth naming.

Install

Grab the code from GitHub, then:

  • Firefox — open about:debugging → This Firefox → Load Temporary Add-on → pick manifest.json
  • Chrome — open chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked → pick the folder

Bring your own key

There is no server and no account. You paste an API key in Settings, and the extension talks to that provider directly from your browser.

A summary costs well under a cent, and every one is cached, so you pay once per video ever.

Privacy

Your key is stored in your browser and sent only to the provider you chose. Nothing goes to any server of mine, because there isn't one.

When you click Worth it?, that video's title, channel and transcript are sent to your chosen provider. Nothing is sent until you click, and nothing else is sent at all. Summaries are cached locally in your browser.
